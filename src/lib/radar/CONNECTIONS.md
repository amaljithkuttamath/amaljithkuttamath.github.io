# Source-checked connections

`connections.ts` contains a small editorial selection, not an automatically inferred citation graph. Each entry requires two identifiable papers, their first publication dates, a specific relationship, a short reason, and an explicit passage in the later paper. Review the full text before adding an edge. Similar titles, shared topics, citations without context, scores, and co-mentions are insufficient.

The current examples were checked on 2026-10-24:

- FlashVSR+ §3.1 explicitly retains FlashVSR's three-stage framework and changes attention and distillation.
- Sage-A1.5 §1 explicitly names Agents-A1 as its base.
- Looking Ahead Appendix C.1 critiques chained prediction in Next Forcing. It explicitly declares independent, concurrent development. The interface therefore calls this a design challenge, not a direct lineage or a replicated disproof.

URLs are version-pinned where the passage depends on a particular version. Keep quotations short, name the section, and distinguish an author's argument from independently verified results. The displayed timeline is a publication-date cutoff over today's reviewed selection, not a reconstruction of what Radar knew on that date. The separate Edition activity view preserves historical mentions and traction.

Unreviewed work gets no edge. Additions should include source review and boundary tests for publication dates; never invent a connection to fill an empty view.
